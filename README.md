# Supply Chain Visibility and Optimization

## Project Overview

The **Supply Chain Visibility and Optimization** project uses Power BI to analyze supply chain operations and provide data-driven insights for better business decision-making.

The project covers data modeling, inventory analytics, delivery performance, supplier performance, transportation analytics, warehouse efficiency, and executive-level reporting.

## Objectives

* Analyze supply chain data and identify important trends.
* Monitor inventory and delivery performance.
* Evaluate supplier reliability, quality, and performance.
* Analyze transportation and logistics efficiency.
* Monitor warehouse utilization and productivity.
* Provide consolidated executive-level KPIs and insights.

## Project Modules

### 1. Data Modelling

Created a structured data model using data cleaning, transformation, and a star schema approach.

### 2. Inventory & Delivery Analytics

Analyzed inventory performance, stock levels, inventory turnover, delivery performance, and late delivery risk.

### 3. Supplier & Transportation Analytics

Developed supplier scorecards, supplier ranking, supplier benchmarking, transportation analysis, and shipping-mode performance analysis.

### 4. Warehouse & Executive Analytics

Analyzed warehouse utilization, throughput, productivity, and developed a consolidated executive dashboard with key supply chain KPIs.

## Key Outcomes

The project provides a consolidated view of supply chain performance across inventory, delivery, suppliers, transportation, and warehouse operations.
The dashboards enable users to monitor KPIs, identify performance issues, compare operational areas, and support data-driven supply chain decisions.
